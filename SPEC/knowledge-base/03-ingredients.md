# Ingredients Information

## Our Ingredient Philosophy

We believe in transparent labeling. Every ingredient is chosen for its efficacy and safety.

## Key Ingredients We Use

### Hyaluronic Acid
A powerful humectant that draws moisture into the skin.
- **Benefits:** Intense hydration, plumping effect
- **Suitable for:** All skin types

### Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
Powerful antioxidant that brightens and protects.
- **Benefits:** Brightening, anti-aging, sun damage protection
- **Suitable for:** Dull, aging, hyperpigmented skin

### Niacinamide
Vitamin B3 that strengthens and balances.
- **Benefits:** Minimizes pores, regulates oil, evens tone
- **Suitable for:** All skin types, especially oily

### Retinol
Anti-aging powerhouse that cell turnover.
- **Benefits:** Reduces fine lines, wrinkles, texture
- **Suitable for:** Aging skin (start slowly)

### Peptides
Amino acid chains that boost collagen.
- **Benefits:** Firming, anti-aging, skin repair
- **Suitable for:** Mature skin

### Ceramides
Skin-identical lipids that strengthen barrier.
- **Benefits:** Moisture retention, barrier repair
- **Suitable for:** Dry, sensitive skin

### Salicylic Acid
Beta hydroxy acid for exfoliation.
- **Benefits:** Unclogs pores, reduces breakouts
- **Suitable for:** Oily, acne-prone skin

### Glycolic Acid
Alpha hydroxy acid for surface exfoliation.
- **Benefits:** Brightening, texture improvement
- **Suitable for:** Most skin types (patch test first)

## Ingredient Safety

### Free From
- Parabens
- Sulfates
- Phthalates
- Synthetic fragrances
- Petrochemicals
- Formaldehyde donors

### Natural Preservatives
We use ECOCERT-approved preservatives:
- Sodium benzoate
- Potassium sorbate
- Benzyl alcohol

## Patch Testing

We recommend patch testing new products:
1. Apply small amount to inner forearm
2. Wait 24 hours
3. If no reaction, use as directed
