# Products Information

## Product Categories

Our products are organized into several categories:

- **Skincare:** Cleansers, moisturizers, serums, masks
- **Body Care:** Body lotions, scrubs, oils
- **Sets & Bundles:** Gift sets and value bundles

## How to Use Our Products

### Step 1: Cleanse

Start with a gentle cleanser appropriate for your skin type.

### Step 2: Treat

Apply serums or treatments targeting your specific concerns.

### Step 3: Moisturize

Lock in hydration with a suitable moisturizer.

### Step 4: Protect

Finish with sunscreen during daytime hours.

## Product Quality

- All products are manufactured in FDA-compliant facilities
- Each batch is tested for purity and potency
- Shelf life: 24 months from manufacture date
- Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
